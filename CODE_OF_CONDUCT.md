# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participation in this project is expected to be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the software. The project welcomes people with different levels of experience and backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Give specific, actionable feedback about code and design decisions.
- Explain the impact of a problem without attacking a person.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, private messages, or personal information.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct inaccurate statements.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate intimidation or repeated unwanted contact.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or abusive language.
- Knowingly sharing malware, credential theft attempts, or malicious code.

## Maintainer response

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when conduct violates this policy. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and focused on keeping the project safe and productive.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact route listed in the repository profile. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports. Include relevant links and a factual description. Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and other project spaces operated for this repository.
